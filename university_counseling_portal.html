Year 12 Application Timeline

Key benchmarks throughout your senior academic year.

Fall (Sep - Nov)

Finalizing & Early Apps

  • Finalize list (3 Safety, 3 Match, 2 Reach).
  • Request Teacher LORs (Letters of Recommendation).
  • Draft Personal Statements & Essays.
  • Submit Early Action/Decision & Oxbridge (Oct/Nov).
Winter (Dec - Feb)

Regular Deadlines

  • Submit Regular Decision Applications (Jan 1-15).
  • Submit UCAS equal consideration applications (Jan 31).
  • Complete Financial Aid forms (FAFSA / CSS Profile).
  • Send mid-year transcripts to universities.
Spring (Mar - May)

Decisions & Enrollment

  • Receive decisions and financial aid offers.
  • Attend offer-holder days & virtual tours.
  • Submit final deposit by May 1 (National Decision Day).
  • Finalize student visas and housing bookings.
